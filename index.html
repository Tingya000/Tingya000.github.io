1. Problem

Information Overload & Lack of Interpretation

  • Retail investors face excessive financial data, including EPS, technicals, news, and market sentiment.
  • Most lack the ability to identify what truly matters.
  • Many rely on market sentiment or follow trends blindly.

Active ETFs are transparent, but not understandable

Investors can see holdings, but cannot interpret:

  • Why positions are added
  • Whether changes are significant
  • If it is worth following
Key Problem: Professional investment decisions exist in public data, but retail investors cannot translate them into usable insights.

2. Solution

A Platform that Translates ETF Manager Behavior into Signals

We convert raw ETF holdings into structured, interpretable investment insights.

Behavior Tracking

  • New positions
  • Position increases
  • Reductions
  • Full exits

Signal Scoring System

  • Magnitude of change
  • ETF performance quality
  • Multi-ETF consensus
  • Position significance

Multi-layer Filtering

  • Fundamental indicators, such as EPS and revenue growth
  • Valuation metrics
  • Technical trends
Not copying trades — interpreting decisions.
