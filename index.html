Market Problem

Retail investors do not need more data. They need interpretation.

Today’s investors are surrounded by EPS numbers, technical indicators, news headlines, financial reports, and social media opinions. The problem is not the lack of information. The real problem is that most retail investors do not know which information matters, how to connect different signals, or when a market move is simply noise.

Information Overload

Financial websites and brokerage apps provide massive amounts of data, but they often leave the interpretation work to the user. For beginners, this creates confusion rather than confidence.

Blind Trend Following

Many investors follow market sentiment, social media discussions, or short-term price movements without understanding the underlying reason behind a trade.

Unclear Professional Signals

Active ETF holdings are public, but most investors cannot tell whether a new position, a large increase, or a full exit represents a meaningful investment signal.

Professional investment decisions exist in public data, but retail investors cannot translate them into usable insights.
Solution

A platform that translates ETF manager behavior into signals.

FundSight converts raw active ETF holdings into structured and interpretable investment insights. The platform does not encourage users to blindly copy trades. Instead, it helps them understand the behavior behind professional investment decisions and evaluate whether those actions are meaningful.

Behavior Tracking

The system identifies whether ETF managers are creating new positions, increasing exposure, reducing holdings, or fully exiting a stock. Each action is treated as a behavioral signal rather than a simple data point.

Signal Scoring

FundSight evaluates the magnitude of holding changes, the quality of the ETF, the consistency across multiple ETFs, and the significance of the position within the portfolio.

Multi-layer Filtering

ETF behavior is combined with EPS growth, revenue growth, valuation metrics, and technical trends to reduce the risk of following weak or noisy signals.

FundSight is not a copy-trading tool. It is a decision-support platform that explains professional behavior.
Core Engine

Target Users

Designed for investors who want clarity before action.

FundSight first targets beginner and young retail investors who want to invest but lack the time or knowledge to conduct complete research. It also serves intermediate investors who want a faster way to identify professional money movement and potential stock ideas. In the long run, the same analysis engine can be integrated into broker platforms, banks, wealth management systems, and financial education products.

Validation

Starting with MVP design and signal backtesting.

The first stage of FundSight focuses on validating whether active ETF behavior contains useful predictive information. The platform will analyze selected active ETFs, track their holding changes over time, and test whether certain behaviors are followed by stronger stock performance.

The key assumption is that stocks bought by multiple high-performing ETFs may contain stronger signals than stocks bought by only one fund. Large position increases may also reflect stronger manager conviction, while new positions may reveal fresh investment opportunities earlier than existing holdings.

Business Model

From free signal discovery to premium intelligence.

FundSight adopts a freemium model. The free version helps users discover basic ETF holding changes and simple signal categories. The premium version provides deeper analytics, scoring details, ETF manager consensus, advanced filters, and personalized watchlists.

Freemium

Basic ETF tracking and simple signal discovery lower the entry barrier and attract retail investors who are new to financial analysis.

Subscription

Monthly or annual plans unlock advanced scoring, premium insights, and deeper manager behavior analysis.

B2B Integration

The signal engine can be provided to brokers, financial platforms, banks, and investment education providers through API or white-label services.
