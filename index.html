FundSight

Turning active ETF holdings into actionable investment signals. Not just what professional investors buy, but how and why their decisions matter.

The Problem

In modern financial markets, information is no longer scarce. Retail investors are constantly exposed to earnings reports, valuation metrics, technical indicators, and an overwhelming stream of financial news. While this abundance of data may seem beneficial, it often creates confusion rather than clarity. Most investors do not struggle with access to information, but with the ability to interpret it.

As a result, many individuals rely on fragmented signals or short-term trends. Some follow social media discussions, while others react to price movements without understanding the underlying drivers. This behavior frequently leads to inconsistent decision-making and unnecessary risk.

Active exchange-traded funds (ETFs) introduce an interesting paradox. Their holdings are transparent and publicly available, yet the reasoning behind those holdings remains difficult to interpret. Investors can observe which stocks are included in a portfolio, but they cannot easily determine whether a position represents a new strategic move, a minor adjustment, or a declining conviction.

Professional investment decisions are already embedded in public data, but most investors lack the tools to translate those decisions into meaningful insights.

The Solution

FundSight is designed to bridge the gap between raw financial data and practical investment understanding. Instead of presenting ETF holdings as static lists, the platform focuses on the dynamic behavior of professional managers. It interprets how positions evolve over time and translates these changes into structured signals that can be understood by non-professional investors.

The core idea is simple: investment behavior carries more meaning than isolated data points. When a fund manager initiates a new position, significantly increases exposure, or fully exits a stock, these actions reflect underlying judgment. FundSight captures these behavioral patterns and converts them into a consistent framework that allows users to evaluate their importance.

To ensure that these signals are not misleading, the system integrates additional layers of analysis. Fundamental indicators such as earnings growth and revenue trends are considered alongside valuation metrics and technical conditions. This multi-dimensional approach reduces noise and helps distinguish between meaningful signals and temporary market fluctuations.

FundSight does not encourage investors to copy trades. Instead, it provides a structured way to understand how professionals think and act.

How It Works

The platform begins by collecting and organizing data from selected active ETFs. It tracks not only current holdings but also historical changes, allowing the system to identify how positions evolve over time. This temporal perspective is essential for understanding whether a stock is gaining importance within a portfolio or gradually being reduced.

Once the data is structured, FundSight analyzes each position to detect meaningful behavioral changes. These include the creation of new positions, increases in allocation, reductions in exposure, and complete exits. Each type of action is interpreted differently, reflecting varying levels of confidence and strategic intent.

The next stage involves transforming these observations into a scoring system. By evaluating the magnitude of change, the relative importance of the position, and the consistency across multiple ETFs, the platform assigns a signal strength that reflects the degree of conviction behind a decision.

Finally, the system applies additional filters based on financial fundamentals and market behavior. This ensures that the resulting insights are not only based on professional actions but also supported by broader economic and technical conditions. The output is presented in a simplified form, allowing users to quickly identify whether a stock represents a strong signal, a potential opportunity, or a higher-risk situation.

Target Users

FundSight is primarily designed for retail investors who seek clarity in an increasingly complex market environment. Many of these users are either new to investing or do not have the time to conduct detailed financial analysis. For them, the platform provides a structured way to access professional-level insights without requiring extensive expertise.

At the same time, the system is also valuable for more experienced investors who want to streamline their research process. By highlighting areas where professional capital is being actively deployed, FundSight can serve as a starting point for deeper analysis.

In the long term, the underlying technology can be extended to institutional applications. Financial platforms, brokerage services, and educational tools can integrate these insights to enhance their offerings and provide users with more meaningful guidance.

Market Opportunity

The rise of ETF investing has fundamentally changed how individuals participate in financial markets. As access to investment products becomes easier, the demand for tools that simplify decision-making continues to grow. However, most existing solutions either provide raw data or automate decisions without transparency.

FundSight occupies a unique position between these two extremes. It does not overwhelm users with unstructured information, nor does it obscure decision-making behind opaque algorithms. Instead, it focuses on interpretability, helping users understand the reasoning behind investment actions.

The future of investing is not about more data, but about better understanding.

Conclusion

FundSight transforms the way investors interact with financial information. By focusing on behavior rather than static data, it reveals patterns that are otherwise difficult to detect. Through a combination of data analysis, structured interpretation, and clear presentation, the platform enables users to make more informed and confident decisions.

Ultimately, the goal is not to replicate professional trades, but to understand the logic behind them. In doing so, FundSight empowers investors to move beyond reactive behavior and develop a more disciplined and thoughtful approach to the market.

From data, to behavior, to better decisions.
